Summer Whites & Wine

Some weekends you plan. Others you remember forever.

See Packages & Book

Summer Whites & Wine: Charlotte Weekend 2026 is an intimate women's getaway built around the things that actually matter — good wine, good people, meaningful conversation, and the kind of unhurried moments we don't get enough of in everyday life.

We're taking over Uptown Charlotte for a long weekend of live jazz, North Carolina wine country, champagne, and the kind of experiences that make a trip feel like more than just a trip. Think getting dressed up for no reason other than you wanted to. Sitting across from someone new and realizing you could talk for hours. Sipping something beautiful while the music plays and the evening slows down.

Summer Whites & Wine was designed to feel indulgent without being overwhelming — a soft landing from real life with the right women in a beautiful city.

Friday night, we gather in the hotel lobby for a champagne toast, glasses up, all white everything, before stepping out together for a live jazz experience that sets the tone for the entire weekend. It's the kind of entrance you plan an outfit around.

Saturday, we head into North Carolina wine country together for a full day of wine, conversation, laughter, and beautiful scenery. Guests also have the option to add an elevated caviar and wine pairing experience to their Saturday itinerary.

Sunday is yours to close out however feels right — whether that means joining us for an optional farewell brunch, exploring the city a little longer, or taking one last slow moment before heading home.

However long you join us, you'll leave full of wine, laughter, connection, and the kind of memories that come up in conversation for years.

Charlotte is calling.
Come exactly as you are.
— Kiu & Ro

The Details

Hotel

Hyatt House Charlotte Center City
A centrally located, upscale extended-stay hotel in the heart of Uptown Charlotte, walkable to dining, nightlife, and everything the weekend has to offer.

Parking

Valet parking available at the hotel for $55/night. Self-parking option available across the street at the Midnight Diner parking lot.

Please note: the hotel requires a refundable $75 incidental authorization hold at check-in.

Important to Know

All experiences are ticketed and reserved exclusively for registered guests. Out of respect for the intimacy of this experience and the women who have secured their space, we're unable to accommodate walk-ins or drop-ins at any point during the weekend.

Flexible payment arrangements are available. Estimated package pricing may vary slightly based on occupancy preferences and final selections. Additional timing and event details will be shared with registered guests closer to the experience.

All payments made toward Summer Whites & Wine: Charlotte Weekend 2026 are non-refundable and non-transferable.

Travel insurance is strongly recommended to help protect your investment in the event of unexpected cancellations, delays, emergencies, or unforeseen circumstances.

What's Included

✕ Not Included

The Itinerary

Day 1 — Friday

Welcome toast, all-white night of jazz with small bites and cocktails. Transportation included.

Day 2 — Saturday

Full day winery experience with charcuterie, plus an optional caviar add-on. Transportation included.

Day 3 — Sunday (Optional)

For those who want to stretch the weekend just a little longer, there's a suggested meetup at one of two Charlotte favorites: The Asbury or Sixty Vines.
