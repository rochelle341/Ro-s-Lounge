Napa Valley

The Bucket List Weekend

Some places aren't just a destination. They're the reason bucket lists exist.

See Packages & Book

You've talked about Napa Valley for years. Watched friends go. Saved the pictures. Told yourself, "One day."

This is the weekend that turns "one day" into "I'm going."

The Bucket List Weekend was created for women who are ready to stop talking about Napa Valley and finally experience it for themselves. You don't have to figure out where to stay, which wineries to visit, or how to plan the perfect itinerary. Just pack your bags, bring your sense of adventure, and get ready to experience Napa Valley the way you've always imagined.

You'll stay at a thoughtfully selected hotel in the heart of Napa Valley, conveniently located near many of the experiences that make this destination so unforgettable. Each morning begins with a complimentary breakfast before setting out to explore wine country, every detail already taken care of.

The Weekend

Friday

All Black Everything

Your Bucket List Weekend begins in the heart of Downtown Napa with our Welcome Dinner. Think All Black Everything — a dress, a jumpsuit, a skirt, or your favorite evening look. We'll gather over incredible food, smooth R&B, great wine, and an atmosphere that sets the tone for everything about to unfold. When dinner ends, the night doesn't have to — explore Downtown Napa with the group or head back to relax before another unforgettable day in wine country.

Saturday

Black-Owned & Unforgettable

Relax with private chauffeured transportation as your journey winds through Napa Valley, discovering exceptional Black-owned wineries, thoughtfully paired small bites, remarkable wines, breathtaking vineyard views, and the stories behind every bottle. We'll stop at the iconic Napa Valley Welcome Sign for the photo that says, "I finally made it."

Sunday

The Legend Itself

Step aboard the legendary Napa Valley Wine Train for an unforgettable six-hour journey through the heart of wine country. Begin with a welcome glass of sparkling wine, settle into beautifully restored vintage railcars, and enjoy a gourmet four-course meal prepared fresh onboard. Step off at three distinctive winery destinations for guided tastings, then relax in the open-air railcar as vineyard views stretch for miles, with music, dancing, and timeless elegance the whole way.

Optional VIP Arrival Experience

Arrive Thursday. Experience more.

Thursday, October 7, 2027  •  3-Night Package Only

An Extraordinary Beginning

For those who believe one bucket list experience deserves another, your Napa Valley story begins one day earlier. With private chauffeured transportation, you'll step inside the blending lab and work alongside wine professionals to create your own signature wine — blend it, bottle it, design your own personalized label, and leave with a handcrafted keepsake that's uniquely yours.

A lab coat is provided for the blending experience, so just pack comfortable flats. Afterward, slip into your favorite heels as the evening transitions into an elegant cocktail hour with exceptional wines, beautifully presented hors d'oeuvres, and an upscale dining experience in one of Napa Valley's most memorable settings.

Requires a minimum of 10 guests. If the minimum isn't met, the VIP Arrival Experience won't be offered and all VIP payments are fully refunded — your Bucket List Weekend reservation stays unchanged either way. Not available with the 2-night package, and cannot be purchased as a standalone experience.
